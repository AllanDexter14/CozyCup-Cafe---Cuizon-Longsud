About CozyCup Café

Founded right inside the heart of Palawan State University in Puerto Princesa City, CozyCup Café started as a passion project by two CS students, Jhon and Allan. We wanted to build a warm space where students and faculty alike can recharge with quality coffee, fuel up with snacks, and catch up on group projects without breaking the bank.
